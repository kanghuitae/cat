Bring your cat home

macOS

  1. Open CatApp.dmg and drag CatApp into Applications
  2. On first launch, confirm your language. English is the default; Korean or Japanese is suggested when detected on your device
  3. Open the app and find 🐱 in your menu bar — your cat’s control panel
  4. For notification fetching, enable CatApp in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility

Windows

  1. Select Get in the Microsoft Store to install Desktop Cat
  2. Open the app and find 🐱 in the system tray

All set. Your first cat is already making itself at home. 🐾
