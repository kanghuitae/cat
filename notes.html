Release notes

What’s new in Desktop Cat for macOS.

Upcoming: v1.9.20

September 5, 2026

This update is not available yet. The current macOS download is v1.9.19.

  • Choose your language on first launch. English is the default. If your device prefers Korean or Japanese, the setup screen suggests it and asks you to confirm. Existing language preferences are preserved.
  • More reliable updates. Downloaded installers are saved before their temporary files expire. The replacement app is verified and staged before the old app is moved, with rollback if installation fails.
  • Display recovery. A temporary loss of displays during sleep no longer discards scenes. The primary display stays tied to the menu bar rather than keyboard focus.
  • An English website. Home, release notes and privacy policy now open in English, with Korean available through the language links. Adoption copy now explains that you choose each breed.
